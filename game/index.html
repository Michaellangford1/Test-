    For parents & teachers

    Pip's Super Quest practises the Year 1 curriculum for England. Each world gets harder:

    • Meadow: Phase 3 digraphs (ch, sh, th, ng, ai, ee, oa, oo), counting to 10, bonds to 10, adding within 10, 2D shapes.
    • Desert: Phase 3 trigraphs and vowel digraphs (igh, ar, or, ur, ow, oi, ear, air, er), counting to 20, subtracting, counting in 2s, 5s and 10s.
    • Snow: Phase 5 alternative graphemes (ay, ou, ie, ea, oy, ir, aw, ph, ew, ue), bonds to 20, tens and ones, 3D shapes.
    • Crystal: split digraphs (a‑e, i‑e, o‑e, u‑e), missing number problems, numbers to 100.

    Every level includes real or alien words in the style of the Phonics Screening Check. Children get a second try, then the answer is shown and explained. Scores below count answers that were right first time.
